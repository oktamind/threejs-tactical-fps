Relay Yard: a round-based tactical FPS in three.js. Plant the charge at site A or B, or eliminate all four defenders, before the clock runs out — with a buy menu, four weapons (rifle, SMG, pistol and a one-shot-headshot sniper), a Kevlar vest that soaks 60% of every hit, frag grenades, sprint and aim-down-sights with a sniper scope, and three defender types (patrolling Defenders, rushing Rushers, long-range Marksmen) that patrol, hear gunfire, converge to defuse, and get tougher every round. Synthesised WebAudio sound, muzzle flash, tracers, screen shake, a dusk sky with stars and a low sun, floating site labels, a sweeping radar dish over the yard, and a match-results screen with a round-by-round history.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```
